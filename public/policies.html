Community Guidelines

ShrekBook is a social platform where users can communicate, publish content, and interact with other members.

Users are expected to use the platform responsibly and treat other members respectfully.

Prohibited Content

  • Illegal content or activities
  • Credible threats of violence
  • Targeted harassment or abuse
  • Spam and malicious activity
  • Scams and phishing
  • Malware or malicious code
  • Sexually explicit content
  • Sexual exploitation material
  • Deceptive impersonation
  • Content that violates applicable laws

Moderation Policy

ShrekBook may take action against accounts or content that violates these policies.

Possible Actions

  • Warning
  • Content removal
  • Temporary restrictions
  • Kick or temporary account restriction
  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent account ban
  • Email or account restrictions where appropriate

Moderation decisions may consider the severity and frequency of violations.

Privacy

ShrekBook may collect information necessary to operate accounts, authenticate users, provide features, maintain security, prevent abuse, and operate the platform.

Users should never publicly share passwords, authentication tokens, financial information, or other sensitive information.

ShrekBook may use cookies or similar technologies for login sessions, security, functionality, analytics, and other website operations.

Third-party services used by ShrekBook may independently process information according to their own policies.

Advertising

ShrekBook may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services.

Advertisements may use technologies such as cookies or similar technologies depending on the advertising provider.

ShrekBook may attempt to restrict inappropriate advertising categories where the advertising provider provides such controls.

Accounts

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and for activity performed through their account.

Accounts may be restricted or terminated when they are used to violate ShrekBook's policies or Terms of Service.

Changes to These Policies

ShrekBook may update these policies as the website, community, and features develop.

Updated policies will be published on this page.
