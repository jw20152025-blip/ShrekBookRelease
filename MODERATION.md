ShrekBook Moderation System

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This document explains ShrekBook's moderation actions, how users may receive them, and what happens when each action is applied.

ShrekBook uses several levels of moderation. The action taken depends on the severity of the violation, the user's previous moderation history, and the circumstances surrounding the incident.

1. Content Takedown 🗑️

A Content Takedown removes a specific piece of content from ShrekBook.

This can apply to:

Posts
Comments
Images
Profile content
Other user-generated content
How can you receive one?

A Content Takedown may occur when content:

Violates ShrekBook's rules.
Contains spam.
Harasses or targets another user.
Exposes another person's private information.
Contains malicious or harmful material.
Violates the Terms of Service.
What happens to you?

The affected content is removed or hidden from other users.

Your account is not automatically suspended when a Content Takedown occurs.

Depending on the violation, you may also receive a warning or another moderation action.

2. Kick 👢

A Kick is a temporary removal of a user from an active ShrekBook session.

A kick is less severe than a ban and is primarily intended to immediately stop disruptive behavior.

How can you receive one?

A moderator may kick a user for:

Disruptive behavior.
Spam.
Repeatedly breaking chat rules.
Continuing inappropriate behavior after being asked to stop.
Other situations where immediate removal is appropriate.
What happens to you?

When kicked:

Your current session may be terminated.
You may be returned to the login page.
You may be prevented from immediately continuing the activity that caused the kick.

A kick does not normally delete your account or permanently prevent you from returning.

You may be able to log back in unless another moderation action has also been applied.

3. Temporary Ban 🔨

A Temporary Ban prevents an account from accessing ShrekBook for a specified period.

How can you receive one?

Temporary bans may be issued for:

Repeated rule violations.
Serious harassment.
Repeated spam.
Repeated inappropriate content.
Attempting to bypass previous moderation actions.
Serious but non-permanent violations.

The length of a temporary ban depends on the severity and history of the violation.

What happens to you?

While temporarily banned:

You cannot use your account normally.
You may be prevented from logging in.
Your existing account data generally remains intact.
Your public content may remain available or may be removed depending on the violation.
The ban expires after its specified duration unless further action is taken.

Creating another account to avoid a temporary ban may be considered ban evasion and can result in additional moderation action.

4. Permanent Ban ⛔

A Permanent Ban prevents an account from returning to ShrekBook.

How can you receive one?

Permanent bans may be issued for severe or repeated violations, including:

Serious harassment or threats.
Deliberately exposing private information.
Repeated ban evasion.
Serious abuse of ShrekBook's systems.
Attempts to compromise accounts or databases.
Malicious attacks against ShrekBook.
Repeated serious violations of the Terms of Service.

A permanent ban may also be issued immediately for particularly severe behavior.

What happens to you?

When permanently banned:

Your account can no longer be used normally.
Login access may be disabled.
You may be prevented from creating replacement accounts.
Your public content may be removed, hidden, or retained depending on the circumstances.
Relevant moderation records may be retained.

A permanent ban does not necessarily mean your account is immediately deleted.

Account deletion is a separate moderation action.

5. Account Deletion 🗑️

Account Deletion permanently removes an account from ShrekBook.

This is the most severe account-level action.

How can an account be deleted?

An account may be deleted when:

A permanent ban is accompanied by account deletion.
The account is involved in severe abuse.
The account presents a significant security risk.
The account is used for malicious activity.
Deletion is necessary to protect ShrekBook or its users.
The account is otherwise eligible for deletion under ShrekBook's policies.

Account deletion may also occur when a user voluntarily requests deletion, where ShrekBook supports user-requested deletion.

What happens to you?

When your account is deleted:

Your account is permanently removed or disabled.
You can no longer log in using that account.
Your profile may disappear.
Your posts, comments, reactions, and other associated content may be deleted, anonymized, or removed depending on how ShrekBook's systems handle deletion.
Account-related information may be removed from active databases.
Some information may remain temporarily in backups or logs.
Certain information may be retained where necessary for security, abuse prevention, legal obligations, or other legitimate purposes.

Account deletion is generally irreversible.

6. How Moderation Escalates 📈

Moderation does not always follow a fixed progression.

A typical progression for repeated minor violations may look like:

Content Takedown
↓
Warning
↓
Kick
↓
Temporary Ban
↓
Permanent Ban
↓
Account Deletion

However, ShrekBook may skip steps when a violation is sufficiently serious.

For example:

Severe security attack → Permanent Ban / Account Deletion

rather than:

Warning → Kick → Temporary Ban

7. Multiple Violations

Repeated violations may result in increasingly serious actions.

For example:

First violation → Content Takedown

Repeated violation → Warning

Continued violations → Temporary Ban

Further violations → Permanent Ban

Moderation history may be considered when determining the appropriate action.

8. Ban Evasion 🚫

Attempting to avoid a ban by creating or using another account may result in additional moderation action.

For example:

Account A → Permanently banned

Account B created to bypass the ban → Account B may also be banned

Ban evasion may result in a longer or permanent restriction.

9. Appeals ⚖️

Where an appeal system is available, users may appeal moderation actions.

An appeal should explain:

What happened.
Which account was affected.
Which moderation action was applied.
Why the user believes the action was incorrect.
Any relevant information that may help the review.

An appeal does not automatically reverse a moderation action.

10. Moderator Actions

Authorized moderators and administrators may apply moderation actions when they believe they are necessary to protect ShrekBook and its users.

Moderators should apply the least severe action reasonably appropriate when circumstances allow.

However, ShrekBook may immediately apply stronger actions when necessary to address serious abuse, security threats, or other severe violations.
