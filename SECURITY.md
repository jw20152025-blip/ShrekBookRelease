Security policy

ShrekBook is still under active development. This article explains ShrekBook's privacy policy.

ShrekBook will store you passwords and emails, however, this is securely kept in ShrekBook's database. We require your emails for the future notification update.

Note:
If you are uncomfortable with recieving emails from ShrekBook or uncomfortable anything else email related, feel welcome to enter a fake email as ShrekBook will
not check the legitimacy of the email.

Warning:
ShrekBook currently has an extremely small development team and henceforth an even smaller support team, so please do not notify us if you would like to change your
emails.

Posts are all public and do not have a private mode. If you post or comment, it will be public. Please post appropriate material. Learn more on MODERATION.md.
